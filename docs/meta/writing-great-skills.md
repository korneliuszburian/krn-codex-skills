# Writing Great Skills

## Purpose

Give one repeated workflow one predictable owner, invocation boundary,
information ladder, and checkable completion contract.

## Invocation

Model-invocable or explicit as `$writing-great-skills`.

## Use and skip

Use for skill creation, rewriting, pruning, trigger routing, metadata,
references, scripts, catalogs, and collisions. Skip ordinary documentation.

## Inputs

Representative positive, nearest-negative, explicit, and composition prompts;
the proposed owner, inputs, output, completion criterion, and invocation mode.

## Output and completion

A minimal validated skill whose descriptions route correctly, resources load
only on their live branch, and fresh forward trials show no systematic early
stop or ownership collision.

## Composition

Uses `$source-to-decision` when external material must justify a mechanism and
`$implement` when deterministic tooling or catalog behavior must change.
