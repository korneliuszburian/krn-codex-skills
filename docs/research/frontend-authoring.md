## P7 topology decision — v2 superseding decision

Status: **implemented topology and routing contract**, decided 2026-09-01.
This supersedes the earlier P7 one-owner candidate, including its conclusion
that discovery and rendered critique could not earn independent public seams.
It preserves P5 `STOP_SAFETY`, the P6 mechanism dispositions above, frozen
inputs, and the rule that routing validation does not prove behavioral value.

Adopt four public surfaces with optional composition, never a required
pipeline:

| Surface | Owns and stops at | Boundary |
|---|---|---|
| `frontend-discovery` | a lawful, observation/inference-separated dossier with hierarchy, art direction, responsive/interaction states, unknowns, and handoff | no build, source mutation, acceptance, or CUBE doctrine; compose generic `grilling`, `research`, or `prototype` only for their named uncertainty |
| `frontend-authoring` | the sole substantial mutable rendered-view outcome and its applicable self-inspection record | consumes a dossier or review packet as input; no discovery/review gate, product acceptance, data/cross-view state, or invented design system |
| `frontend-cube-css` | explicit CUBE responsibility, relation, value, variant, or repair methodology; one already-bounded existing block/composition styling edit | companion for substantial presentation: `frontend-authoring` owns view/page mutation and refinement; standalone CUBE stops after the named local condition and neighbouring check; application state, semantic, shared Global/design-system, and non-presentation ownership return directly to existing host owners |
| `frontend-visual-review` | a read-only, evidence-linked repair packet with category, severity, confidence, condition, expected intent, and owner | no edits, capture-only operation, acceptance, accessibility certification, or fixed-diff Standards/Spec review |

Discovery and visual review pass the deletion test: each has a useful, lawful,
no-edit outcome that can finish without an authoring run. Review hands a bounded
existing-block CUBE repair to `frontend-cube-css`, substantial presentation
repair to `frontend-authoring`, and host-owned state/design-system work to its
existing owner. Refinement remains an authoring input mode, not another
workflow. A browser provider remains observation infrastructure, not a
frontend selector or quality judge. A fixed source diff remains the separate
`code-review` task class.

The retained source dispositions map as follows: CV-01/PS-01/PS-09 support the
authoring baseline and discovery dossier; CV-03/PS-03 support conditional
authoring composition and the explicit CUBE relation lens; CV-04/PS-07/PS-08
support component/state and CUBE layer boundaries; CV-05/CV-06/PS-04–PS-06
support value and cascade scope; CV-07/PS-10/PS-11 support self-inspection and
review evidence limits. CV-02 remains a conditional generic `prototype` use,
not a public frontend workflow. `frontend-process`, `frontend-enforcement`,
and `frontend-library` remain rejected; tokens, utilities, components,
exceptions, and compositions remain references rather than aliases.

The `frontend-cube-css` pack is original operational synthesis from the source ledger and the
cube operational synthesis: it retains Global/Utility/Block/Exception,
composition selection, value/cascade scoping, and repair falsifiers. It rejects
portable absolutes about section elements, JavaScript, frameworks, media
queries, universal tokenization, data-only state, and line limits. It makes no
claim of CUBE superiority or of completeness as a CSS architecture.

P8 is reset to **not started**. Before a behavioral or comparative claim, a new
protocol must freeze task class, permitted writes, development/holdout split,
thresholds, graders, treatment masks, and environment. Existing receipts and
untouched holdouts remain immutable and do not transfer advantage.

The four-surface package is source only, not installed capability state. A
later fixed point may promote only the surfaces admitted by fresh behavioral
proof. Installation of `frontend-cube-css` additionally needs a separately
authorized retirement or migration of the incumbent installed `cube-css`;
this decision grants neither action.

**Reversal falsifier:** reopen topology instead of adding aliases if discovery
or review repeatedly cannot finish without implementation; generic substantial
authoring and explicit CUBE cannot be separated without wrong-owner selection;
review packets lack enough information for bounded repair; or the CUBE companion
causes a second lifecycle/mutation owner. Any such reversal requires a fresh
frozen protocol.
