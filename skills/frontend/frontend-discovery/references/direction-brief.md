# Direction brief and questions

## Dossier shape

Hand off: outcome and audience; primary action; required content in reading
order; observed/reference intent and intentional freedom; art direction;
responsive, content-stress, interaction, and motion states; host seams to
reuse; baseline and state ownership; evidence/acceptance authority; assumptions,
unknowns, non-goals, and next owner.

For each region, use this compact direction record:

| Region | Preserve | Open | Pushback |
|---|---|---|---|
| named region | evidenced hierarchy/relation | lawful undecided trait | explicit simplification of an incompatible responsive treatment |

Pushback is never silent fidelity loss. Add a state matrix recording the
viewport/container, content stress, interaction, loading/error, locale/RTL,
zoom, and motion conditions that matter. List core content, primary action,
source order, and usable reflow first; defer decorative surface, flourish, and
non-evidenced detail until those constraints are settled.

## Ask only decision-changing questions

- Who must understand or do what first, and what is the primary action?
- Which content, hierarchy, and semantic groups are required rather than
  decorative?
- Which source is lawful, what fidelity is intended, and which visual traits
  must survive versus remain open?
- Which route, component, assets, values, localisation, and tests already own
  the seam?
- Which widths/containers, long or missing content, locale/RTL, zoom, input,
  loading/error, and reduced-motion states matter?
- Who can decide unresolved product meaning, brand use, and qualitative
  acceptance?

An unanswered cosmetic preference may inherit a coherent host convention and
be stated as an assumption. An unanswered question about product meaning,
assets, interaction, or acceptance returns to its authority owner.

## Limit and falsifier

The brief does not mandate a build or sequence. It is inadequate when two
reasonable authors could choose incompatible primary action, hierarchy, or
source-use scope; reopen only that decision. Provenance: PS-01, CV-01, CV-07,
and the local frontend-authoring decision synthesis; no generic grilling procedure is
duplicated here.
