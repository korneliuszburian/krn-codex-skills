# Evidence and repair

Name the condition that can change the result: content shape, container or
viewport, zoom, interaction/state, enhancement absence, motion preference, or
source/focus order. Inspect real or stressed content and relevant visible,
keyboard, and focus states. Browser output is evidence of an observed surface,
not a visual-quality, accessibility, performance, or product-acceptance proof.

Repair one owner at a time: semantic baseline, relation, scoped value, utility,
block identity, finite presentation hook, or the application owner. Make the
smallest repair there, rerun the failing condition and one neighbouring
condition, then record: condition, observed evidence, owner, changed boundary,
repair, neighbouring-state result, and external handoff. Escalate a cross-view
token, behavioral/ARIA change, or recurring source-order conflict to its actual
owner rather than masking it with CSS.

## Limit, falsifier, provenance

This is not a required browser, snapshot suite, or conformance audit. If the
same symptom recurs after an owner-level repair, or the repair fixes one
condition while breaking a neighbouring one, the owner or constraint is wrong.
Provenance: CV-07, PS-10, PS-11, WAI evaluation boundaries, and the local
source-to-decision repair method.
