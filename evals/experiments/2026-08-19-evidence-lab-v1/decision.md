# Decision

Decision: **reject exact frozen treatment**

The tested fast/slow gate did not earn adoption. Slow-positive routing declined
from 2/3 to 1/3, P03, P04, and P12 materially regressed, and no repeatable
quality advantage offset those routing failures. Hard-negative routing stayed
correct but cost more under treatment.

Operationally, keep the `source-to-decision` baseline and do not restore the
tested `evidence-lab.md` reference. This rejects only the frozen intervention,
not every possible evidence discipline.

Reopen only with a narrower mechanism and an authoritative grader fixed and
available before execution. A new experiment must be independently
preregistered; it must not revise this disposition.

Non-proof: the sample and post-protocol automated grading do not establish a
general behavioral disadvantage for evidence-lab mechanisms.
